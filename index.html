About me

Hi! I’m Anaid 🇲🇽, originally from Mexico City and now a PhD candidate at UC Berkeley. I earned my MSc in Biological Sciences from the National Autonomous University of Mexico and have spent the past ten years studying black howler monkeys. My main research focus is on how primates adapt their behavior and ecology in human-modified habitats.
